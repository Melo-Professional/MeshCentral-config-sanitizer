MCTools - Config Sanitizer

?

Clean and sanitize your MeshCentral configuration files safely with the Config Sanitizer.

Objective: Automatically remove sensitive data before sharing configs or submitting issues.

How to use:

  • Upload or paste your config.json.
  • Click "Hide Sensitive" to redact secrets.
  • Click "Remove Irrelevant" to clean up noise.
  • Download or Copy the result.
